Record the score. Remember the night.

Games & Feelings keeps your board game nights: what you played, who played, the scores, who won and how it felt. Saving a play takes about as long as putting the box away.

Feelings stay in the words people chose: triumphant, tense, snacky, cautiously optimistic. Wins, ties, team games and cooperative games keep the outcome you pick, never one guessed from the score. Over time, your plays become plain-language stories: who has the edge at which game, and the word your table reaches for most.

Your library stays on your device. There’s no Games & Feelings account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the Android app has no internet access. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app keeps and where.

Questions

Ask in public GitHub issues. Posting requires a GitHub account, and everything you post there is public, so never include your library, an archive, or names, scores or feelings from it.
